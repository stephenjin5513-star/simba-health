# SIMBA Health AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

## Overview
SIMBA Health AI is a personal wellness analytics application that processes WHOOP data with the user's authorization.

## Information Collected
With authorization, the application may access sleep, recovery, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, workout and activity information, and body measurements.

## Use of Information
Information is used to create personal wellness reports and analyze trends. It is not sold or used for advertising.

## Storage and AI Processing
The application is intended to store health records locally on the user's computer. Selected information may be sent to an AI processing provider to generate reports, subject to the provider's applicable privacy terms.

## Retention and Deletion
The user may revoke WHOOP authorization to stop future access and may delete locally stored information. Revocation does not automatically delete previously collected records.

## Security
The application is designed to use authorized access and reasonable security measures.

## Disclaimer
Reports are informational and are not medical diagnoses or a substitute for professional advice.

## Contact
For privacy questions, contact the application operator using the contact information supplied separately.
